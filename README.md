# Insurance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes insurance business performance using Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and MySQL. The objective is to monitor revenue generation, policy sales, opportunities, account executive performance, and business targets through interactive dashboards and KPI reporting.

## Business Problem

Insurance organizations generate large volumes of policy, invoice, brokerage, fee, and opportunity data. Monitoring sales performance and identifying growth opportunities can be challenging without a centralized analytics solution.

This project provides actionable insights to help management track performance, improve revenue generation, and optimize sales operations.

## Tools Used

* MySQL
* Power BI
* Tableau
* Microsoft Excel

## Dataset Overview

The dataset includes:

* Policy Information
* Invoice Data
* Brokerage Data
* Fees Data
* Opportunity Data
* Meeting Records
* Individual Sales Targets
* Account Executive Information

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### KPI 1: Number of Invoices by Account Executive

* Tracks invoice generation performance.
* Compares New, Cross-Sell, and Renewal business.

### KPI 2: Yearly Meeting Count

* Measures client engagement activities.
* Analyzes meeting trends over time.

### KPI 3: Target vs Achievement Analysis

* Revenue Target
* Invoice Value
* Achieved Revenue
* Placement Achievement %
* Invoice Achievement %

### KPI 4: Stage Funnel by Revenue

* Revenue distribution across opportunity stages.
* Identifies high-value sales opportunities.

### KPI 5: Meetings by Account Executive

* Measures executive-level client interactions.
* Evaluates sales engagement performance.

### KPI 6: Opportunity Product Distribution

* Product-wise opportunity analysis.
* Identifies top-performing insurance products.

### KPI 7: Top Open Opportunities

* Tracks high-value pending opportunities.
* Supports sales pipeline management.

## Dashboard Features

* Executive Performance Analysis
* Revenue Funnel Visualization
* Opportunity Tracking
* Target vs Achievement Monitoring
* Product Distribution Analysis
* Interactive Filters and Drilldowns
* KPI Cards and Trend Analysis

## Key Insights

* Revenue performance can be tracked across multiple income classes such as New, Renewal, and Cross-Sell.
* Opportunity funnel analysis highlights stages contributing the highest revenue.
* Meeting activity helps evaluate account executive engagement.
* Product distribution analysis identifies high-demand insurance offerings.
* Target achievement metrics provide visibility into business performance.

## Business Impact

* Improved sales performance tracking.
* Better visibility into revenue opportunities.
* Enhanced executive performance monitoring.
* Data-driven decision-making for insurance operations.
